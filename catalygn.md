## Audience

Owners and operators of small and mid-sized Indian businesses who are stuck at a
revenue plateau, are the bottleneck in their own company, and want a consultant's
thinking applied to their situation.

## Goal

Every post must build authority in business consulting and make the reader want
to reach out. Authority comes from specific, useful, non-obvious insight - not
from self-promotion.

## Pillars

- Positioning: why a business that serves a narrow, specific audience out-earns a generalist, and how to choose that niche
- Pricing: pricing on value delivered rather than hours, protecting margin, and the signals that show you are underpriced
- Sales: consultative selling, qualification, follow-up discipline, and why inbound leads are lost to slow response rather than price
- Operations: turning founder-dependent delivery into documented systems - SOPs, delegation, quality control, capacity planning
- Leadership: hiring for the constraint, effective meetings, feedback that changes behaviour, accountability without micromanagement
- Growth: choosing between channels, sequencing growth bets, and diagnosing why revenue plateaus

## Voice

- Direct and practical. Speak to one business owner, not to an audience.
- Concrete over abstract: name the situation, the mistake, and the fix.
- Confident but never boastful. No hype, no urgency gimmicks, no emojis.
- Never invent statistics, client names, revenue figures, case studies or
  research findings. If a number is not known, do not use one.

## Structure

Every post is Hook -> Body -> CTA:

1. Hook - one line naming a real, specific problem the reader recognises.
2. Body - two to four short paragraphs of practical insight.
3. CTA - ask the reader to DM 9555154334.

## Call to action

The CTA always asks the reader to DM the consulting number directly. Never output
a meeting, calendar or scheduling link. The service guarantees the number is
